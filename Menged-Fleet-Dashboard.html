Menged Fleet Help Center

Quick operational instructions and system guide.

Data Workspace

Use the "Upload data" button in the sidebar or header to import fleet records from Excel/CSV files. Use "Save data" to save all changes locally, or "Export data" to download a complete backup.

Managing Assignments

Click on any vehicle row or click "+ New assignment" to pair a driver, cashier, and POS terminal to a vehicle. Unassigned vehicles are automatically flagged in the attention queue.

Finance Card Audit

Click "+ Add entry" or click "Edit" on any row to add or edit active and corrupt card numbers per cashier. Use "+ Add month / day column" to add custom period dates.
